How I report bugs

A good bug report saves a developer an hour. Mine follow one format: a searchable title, exact steps, what happens against what should happen, the impact, and a suggested fix.

I also list what I confirmed still works, so the fix stays focused and nobody argues about scope. Click through the sample on the right.

Checkout || Coupon Code || Discount applied twice after page refresh Major
  1. Add any item to the cart and open Checkout.
  2. Apply coupon SAVE10.
  3. Refresh the page.
  4. Check the order total.
CurrentThe 10% discount is applied again after refresh, giving 20% off.
ExpectedThe discount is applied once and stays at 10% after refresh.
ImpactCustomers can stack discounts by refreshing, which causes direct revenue loss.
FixCheck whether the coupon is already on the cart server-side before applying it.
  • Coupon applies correctly the first time.
  • Invalid coupons show the correct error.
  • Removing the coupon restores the full price.
